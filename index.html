05
definition

What is an attention span?

Attention span = the length of time you can concentrate on one thing before your brain files a complaint.

▣It can be trained — and right now it's on a losing streak.
▣Average human: ~8 seconds. A goldfish scores ~9. Yes, really.
▣Attention ≠ interest. You're staring. You're not necessarily caring.
▣"Multitasking" doesn't exist. It's just very fast context-switching.

08
history lesson

A brief history of the decline

1450sThe printing press — people read. Focus was a hobby.
1920sRadio & TV — the first background distraction of their era.
1980sRemote controls — we changed channels but never watched.
2000sThe internet — everything, all at once, always.
2007Smartphones — the brain got a leash.
2016+Short-form video — attention span: it's over.
09
reason 01

Short-form content

▣Endless feeds. TikTok, Reels, Shorts — a dopamine hit every 15–60 seconds.
▣The scroll trap. Infinite scroll = your thumb vs. your willpower.
▣Patience reset. Instant gratification rewrote our waiting settings to 0.
▣RIP long videos. A 10-minute YouTube video now feels like a documentary.
10
reason 02

Abundance of content

▣Too many options. Unlimited streaming, feeds, apps, memes. Every second of every day.
▣Choice overload. We can't pick, so we skim. We never actually read.
▣Notification storm. The average person gets ~80–100 pings a day.
▣Snack culture. We snack constantly and never sit down for a meal.
11
reason 03

Dopamine & instant rewards

▣Variable rewards. You never know if the next video is gold — so you never stop.
▣FOMO. Missing out on anything, ever, is now a medical condition.
▣Red-dot panic. A notification is a small red flag your brain treats as a siren.
▣Dopamine treadmill. The bar for "interesting" rises faster than we can chase it.
